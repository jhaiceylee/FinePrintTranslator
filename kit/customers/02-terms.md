## The service

The "Real-World Fine Print" Translator lets the person named in the charter do the job named there. Sample: save a form and see its id.

## What it is not

- It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment.
- It is not legal advice, and it does not say how a case will end.
- It is not a promise of safety, health, or freedom from harm.
- It does not replace a doctor, a lawyer, or emergency services.
- It is not approved by a hospital, a court, or a government office unless a named approval is in the record.

## The person's part

They provide information that is true as far as they know. If the requirements say a person must check a result, they do not rely on it before that check.

## Stopping

Either side may stop using The "Real-World Fine Print" Translator. What is deleted or kept is written in the recovery and retention file, not invented here.
