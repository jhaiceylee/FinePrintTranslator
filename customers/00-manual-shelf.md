## Translate a document

1. Open the Fine Print Translator page.
2. Drop a PDF or photo (or click to choose one).
3. Click **Translate fine print**.
4. Read the three parts and the disclaimer. Check the original document before you rely on the summary.

## Keep a local copy

1. After a result appears, click **Download as text** or **Print**.
2. Save the file or printout on your own device. The site does not keep an account history of past runs.
