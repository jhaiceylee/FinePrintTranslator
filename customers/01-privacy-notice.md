## What is stored

The Fine Print Translator does **not** keep an account history of your uploads or summaries. For a translate request, the file is held in memory long enough to send to Google Gemini and return a result, then discarded by this application. You may download or print the summary to keep it yourself.

## What is sent for translate

The PDF or photo you choose is sent to Google’s Gemini API so the summary can be produced. Google’s terms and privacy practices apply to that processing. This product does not sell your documents.

## What is not collected unless a later decision says so

- Accounts or sign-in profiles
- Payment or card numbers
- A searchable server-side archive of past translations

## Health, safety, and advice

This notice does not make The "Real-World Fine Print" Translator a doctor, a lawyer, or an emergency service. It does not promise that anyone will be safe, healthy, or unharmed. Always check the original document.
