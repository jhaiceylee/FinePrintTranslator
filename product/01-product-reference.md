# Product reference

**Document ID:** FP-PRD-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0 Draft  
**Date:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** Draft. Not approved for external use.

## What the user is buying

Nothing. The Fine Print Translator is free for users (no fee, no free-trial charge). AI use is free via the Google Gemini API free tier. A person drops a photo or PDF—no API key to enter—and gets a short three-part plain-language summary: what the document is, what they must do, and traps, fees, or deadlines. The builder's Gemini key stays off the page; billing stays off. If free capacity is full, the page asks them to try later. The person saves everything on their own end. The product does not store their files or past runs.

## Do not claim

An empty row means the agent asks. The agent does not invent a claim, and it does not delete one. What the product may claim is a human decision.

| Claim | Why it stays out until something changes |
| --- | --- |
| That it gives medical advice or a diagnosis | No doctor or health authority has accepted this claim. |
| That it gives legal advice | No lawyer or court has accepted this claim. |
| That it keeps someone safe or prevents harm | No safety review has accepted this claim. |
| That a hospital, court, or government office has approved it | No such approval is in the record. |
| That it is certified | No certification is named in the record. |
| That it saves money | No agreed saving is in the record. |
| That nobody needs to review the result | A person must still check the original document. |
| That a free cloud AI quota will last forever | Quotas can change; the product rule is that users are not charged and the builder keeps Gemini billing off. |
| That the product will always be available without wait | Free capacity can run out; the page may ask the person to try later. |
